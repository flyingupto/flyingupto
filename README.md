  ### 👨‍🔬 关于我

  我是**北京科技大学**数学专业（图像与图形处理方向）的硕士研究生，预计 **2027 年 7 月**毕业。我的研究处于**优化**与 **AI** 的交叉领域：

  - 🧮 **非凸非光滑优化** —— 分裂算法、Moreau 包络光滑化、稀疏惩罚分位数回归
  - 🎲 **贝叶斯计算与采样** —— MCMC、子空间分裂吉布斯采样、采样–优化对偶
  - 🖼️ **反问题** —— 基于非光滑重尾贝叶斯先验的图像复原
  - 🤖 **多模态 AI Agent** —— 工具增强的视觉语言智能体 + 强化学习（SFT & GRPO、QLoRA），用于工业缺陷检测

  🔭 **我正在寻找 2027 年秋季入学的优化与 AI 方向博士（Ph.D.）机会。** 我喜欢把困难的优化问题转化为可证明收敛、切实可用的算法，也非常乐意合作交流，欢迎随时联系！

  ### 👨‍🔬 About Me

  I am a Master's student in **Mathematics (Image and Graphics Processing)** at USTB, expected to graduate in **July 2027**. My research sits at the intersection of **optimization** and **AI**:

  - 🧮 **Non-convex & non-smooth optimization** — splitting algorithms, Moreau-envelope smoothing, sparsity-penalized quantile regression
  - 🎲 **Bayesian computation & sampling** — MCMC, subspace splitting Gibbs sampling, sampling–optimization duality
  - 🖼️ **Inverse problems** — image restoration under nonsmooth heavy-tailed Bayesian priors
  - 🤖 **Multimodal AI agents** — tool-augmented vision-language agents + reinforcement learning (SFT & GRPO, QLoRA) for industrial defect detection

  🔭 **I am actively seeking a Ph.D. opportunity in Optimization & AI (starting Fall 2027).** I enjoy turning hard optimization problems into provably convergent, practical algorithms — and I would be glad to collaborate or chat. Feel free to reach out!
